How it works

Unkernel boots a Linux kernel compiled to WebAssembly and emulates ARM64 programs in your tab. Cudawg translates a subset of CUDA C to WGSL, then sends kernel launches to your WebGPU adapter. Cargo reads an offline toolchain from image chunks that your browser downloads as needed.
