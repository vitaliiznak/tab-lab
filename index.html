Inside the tab

Each lab boots a WebAssembly Linux kernel with an emulated ARM64 CPU and its own filesystem. The guest sees the files the lab mounts for it. Closing or reloading the page discards its running processes and session files.

The browser can cache toolchain downloads. DuckDB reads data inside Linux, Cargo compiles there, and CUDA kernels reach your GPU through WebGPU.
